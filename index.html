Make it last

Care guide

A few simple habits keep your hair soft, shiny and full.

1. Detangle

Start from the ends and work up, with a wide-tooth comb.

2. Wash gently

Use sulfate-free shampoo and lukewarm water. Don't rub or twist.

3. Condition

Deep condition regularly to keep the hair silky and tangle-free.

4. Store right

Keep on a wig stand or in a satin bag away from heat and dust.
